BMI Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BMI Calculator. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BMI Calculator mobile application.

BMI Calculator provides health and fitness calculations and tracking features, including Body Mass Index (BMI), Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), estimated daily calorie needs, suggested macronutrients, estimated daily water intake, and weight progress tracking.

2. Information We Collect

BMI Calculator does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or password.

Information entered into the application may include:

  • Age
  • Sex
  • Height
  • Weight
  • Activity level
  • Weight goal
  • Weight history
  • Preferred language
  • Weight and height units

3. Local Storage

Information entered into BMI Calculator is stored locally on your device to allow the application to remember your profile, preferences, and weight history.

BMI Calculator does not intentionally upload this information to a developer-controlled server.

You can remove locally stored information by using the data management options available within the application, including the available reset or history-clearing options, or by uninstalling the application.

4. Health Information

BMI Calculator processes information such as age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and weight goals in order to calculate and display health and fitness estimates within the application.

The application may calculate or display BMI, BMR, daily calorie needs, macronutrient suggestions, estimated water intake, healthy weight ranges, and weight progress.

This information is processed locally on the user's device and is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

Important: BMI Calculator does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice. BMI, calorie, BMR, macronutrient, water, and healthy-weight results are estimates and should not be considered a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

5. Intended Users

BMI Calculator is intended for adults aged 18 years and older.

The application is not intended to provide health, medical, weight-management, or nutrition guidance for children or adolescents.

BMI Calculator should not be used as a substitute for professional guidance during pregnancy, breastfeeding, or for the management of medical conditions.

6. Weight History

BMI Calculator allows users to record weight measurements and view their weight progress over time.

Weight history is stored locally on the user's device and is not intentionally transmitted to developer-controlled servers.

Users can clear their stored weight history through the application's available data-management options.

7. Application Preferences

BMI Calculator allows users to customize certain application preferences, such as language, weight units, and height units.

These preferences are stored locally on the user's device so that the application can remember their selected settings.

8. Internet Connection

The main calculations and core features of BMI Calculator are designed to work locally on the device and do not require an internet connection.

Certain operating-system features, such as sharing the application or opening external links, may require an internet connection.

9. Advertising and Third-Party Services

BMI Calculator may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Meta Audience Network.

These advertising providers may collect or process certain information from your device in order to deliver, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on applicable laws, device settings, and the advertising provider's policies, information used for advertising purposes may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, IP address, app and device information, and information about interactions with advertisements.

The health and fitness information that you enter into BMI Calculator, such as your age, sex, height, weight, activity level, weight goals, and weight history, is not intentionally sent to our servers for advertising purposes.

Third-party advertising providers operate under their own privacy policies. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of the relevant advertising providers to understand how information may be collected and used.

Meta Audience Network may provide different advertising formats and monetization solutions for mobile applications.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize the collection and transmission of personal information.

Because BMI Calculator stores user-entered information locally, users should also protect access to their device.

11. Children's Privacy

BMI Calculator is intended for adults and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to BMI Calculator, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

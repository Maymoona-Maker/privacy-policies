FitCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FitCalc. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the FitCalc mobile application.

FitCalc provides health and fitness calculations including BMI, BMR, estimated daily calorie needs, suggested macronutrients, estimated water intake, and weight progress tracking.

2. Information We Collect

FitCalc does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or password.

Information entered into the application may include:

  • Age
  • Sex
  • Height
  • Weight
  • Activity level
  • Weight goal
  • Weight history
  • Application preferences such as language and units

3. Local Storage

Information entered into FitCalc is stored locally on your device to allow the application to remember your profile, preferences, and weight history.

FitCalc does not intentionally upload this information to a developer-controlled server.

You can remove locally stored information by using the data management options available within the application or by uninstalling the application.

4. Health Information

FitCalc processes information such as age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and goals in order to provide calculations within the application.

These calculations are intended for informational and educational purposes only.

Important: FitCalc does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice. BMI, calorie, BMR, macronutrient, and water results are estimates and should not be considered a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

5. Intended Users

FitCalc is intended for adults aged 18 years and older.

The application is not intended to provide health or nutrition guidance for children or adolescents.

FitCalc should not be used as a substitute for professional guidance during pregnancy, breastfeeding, or for the management of medical conditions.

6. Weight History

FitCalc allows users to save weight measurements locally and display progress over time.

Weight history remains stored on the user's device and is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

7. Internet Connection

The main calculations and features of FitCalc are designed to work locally on the device without requiring an internet connection.

Certain external services or operating-system features may require an internet connection when used.

8. Third-Party Services

At the current time, FitCalc does not intentionally send your health-related information to developer-controlled third-party servers.

If third-party services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain how those services handle information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize the collection and transmission of personal information.

Because FitCalc stores user-entered information locally, users should also protect access to their device.

10. Children's Privacy

FitCalc is intended for adults and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to FitCalc or its features.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or FitCalc, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.

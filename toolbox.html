ToolBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ToolBox. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ToolBox mobile application.

ToolBox is designed to provide useful image, PDF, QR code, and text utilities directly on your device.

2. Information We Collect

ToolBox does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

The application does not intentionally collect or store your personal files on our servers.

3. Photos and Files

Some ToolBox features require access to photos or files on your device, such as image compression, image conversion, image resizing, and PDF creation.

Files and photos are processed on your device for the purpose of providing the requested feature. They are not uploaded to our servers.

You control which files or photos you choose to provide to the application through your device's permissions.

4. Camera and QR Scanner

The QR scanning feature may request access to your device's camera.

Camera access is used only to scan QR codes when you choose to use the QR scanner feature.

ToolBox does not continuously access or record your camera.

5. Local Data

Some application preferences and locally generated information may be stored on your device to improve your experience.

This information remains on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

6. Internet Connection

ToolBox is designed so that its main utilities can operate locally on your device without requiring an internet connection.

Certain external services or operating-system features may require an internet connection when used.

7. Third-Party Services

At the current time, ToolBox does not intentionally send your personal files or entered content to third-party servers.

If third-party services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain how those services are used.

8. Children's Privacy

ToolBox is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the application and minimize the collection of personal information.

Because ToolBox primarily processes files locally on your device, your files are not intentionally stored on our servers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or its features.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ToolBox, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.

ToolBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ToolBox. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ToolBox mobile application.

ToolBox is a utility application that provides tools for image processing, PDF creation and processing, QR code scanning and generation, text utilities, and other productivity features.

ToolBox is designed to provide its core utility features directly on your device whenever technically possible.

2. Information We Collect

ToolBox does not require you to create an account. We do not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, password, or other account information in order to use the application.

ToolBox does not intentionally collect or store your personal files on our own servers.

Depending on the feature you choose to use, the application may access information or files on your device that you explicitly select or provide.

3. Photos, Images and Files

Some ToolBox features allow you to select images or files from your device.

These features may require access to photos, media, or files on your device.

Access is used only to provide the feature you requested, such as image editing, cropping, conversion, PDF creation, or other file-related operations.

Files selected by you are not intentionally uploaded to ToolBox servers for storage.

ToolBox does not automatically access your personal photos or files without your interaction with a feature that requires them.

4. Camera and QR Code Scanner

ToolBox includes QR code scanning functionality. When you choose to use the QR scanner, the application may request access to your device camera.

Camera access is used only while you are using the QR scanning feature.

ToolBox does not use the camera to continuously monitor or record you.

5. Permissions

ToolBox may request permissions that are necessary for specific features of the application.

  • Camera: Used for QR code scanning when you choose to use the scanner.
  • Photos / Media: Used when you choose an image from your device.
  • Files / Storage: Used when you choose to open, create, save, or process files.

Permissions are requested only when needed for the relevant functionality.

6. Local Data Storage

ToolBox may store certain application settings and preferences locally on your device, such as user preferences and application settings.

This information is stored locally and is used to provide a better application experience.

7. Sharing and Exporting Files

ToolBox may provide sharing functionality that allows you to share files or generated content using applications installed on your device.

When you choose to share content, the transfer is initiated by you and is handled by the application or service you select through the Android sharing system.

ToolBox does not control the privacy practices of third-party applications that you choose to share content with.

8. Third-Party Services

ToolBox may use third-party libraries and services to provide certain application functionality.

These third-party components may process technical information as necessary to provide their functionality.

Third-party providers are responsible for their own privacy practices and policies.

9. Advertising

ToolBox may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners.

Advertising partners may collect or process certain information from your device, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, depending on their technology and applicable settings.

Such information may be used for purposes including delivering, measuring, and improving advertisements.

Third-party advertising providers operate under their own privacy policies.

For more information about Meta's privacy practices, please review the Meta Privacy Policy .

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

Because ToolBox is primarily designed to process content locally on your device, your personal files are not intentionally stored on ToolBox servers.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

ToolBox is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration because ToolBox does not require users to create accounts.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Links

ToolBox may allow you to open external links or services.

External websites and services have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external services that you choose to visit or use.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

ToolBox does not require an account and therefore does not maintain a user account database containing your personal information.

Information stored locally by the application can generally be removed by deleting the application's local data or uninstalling the application from your device.

Files created or saved by you outside the application's private storage remain under your control and may need to be deleted separately from your device.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, technology, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
